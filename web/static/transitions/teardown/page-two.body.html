Built from the top

On the way in, the order runs backward: whatever left last on the page before lands first here. With Top to bottom, the page assembles itself like it's being typeset, row by row.

A gradient banner

How a trip goes

  1. Click. Every piece on screen is measured where it sits.
  2. Tear down. Pieces fly off, nearest the click first.
  3. Blank. The background goes last, leaving the blank color.
  4. Arrive. The next page starts blank, its pieces waiting off-screen.
  5. Build. They fly in and land, and every style is put back.

Questions

What happens on a refresh?

Nothing. Only a trip that started with an intercepted click builds in.

What if the link doesn't go anywhere?

A download or an empty response leaves this page here, so it builds itself back.

Can a part of the page stay put?

Mark it data-teardown="keep", or try Keep nav bar in the controls.
