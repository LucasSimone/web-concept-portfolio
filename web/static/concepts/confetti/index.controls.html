Shapes ?Which shapes the pieces come in. Each piece picks one at random from the ones switched on, and at least one always stays on. The first five are built in. Heart is a custom shape: a small drawing function defined in this page's own demo.js, the same way you'd add one of your own.
Colors ?The confetti colors, from one up to six. + adds a swatch on the end and − removes the last one. Each piece picks one at random. Its back face is the same color 30% darker, which shows whenever it tumbles over.
