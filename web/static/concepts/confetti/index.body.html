Confetti

Paper confetti on a click. It can burst out of the element you clicked, out of the cursor, or across the whole window. Each piece pops out fast, then slows and flutters down, tumbling so its darker back face flashes as it turns. Element and cursor confetti fly in any direction you give them, or every direction at once.

Your system is set to reduce motion, so the confetti is switched off on this page.

Element

Out of the element

Pieces leave from whichever edges face the way they're going: the top edge for up, the top and right edges for up-right. All around sends each piece straight out from its own edge.

Mouse

Out of the cursor

The burst comes from exactly where you clicked. The direction works the same way as for elements.

Click anywhere in here direction: 'up'
Click anywhere in here direction: 'all'

Screen

Across the whole window

Fired from the edges of the window, whatever was clicked. These buttons are one way to trigger it. In your own code it's one call, Confetti.screen(), say when a form goes through.
