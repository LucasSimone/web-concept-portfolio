Press and hold

Ripple

Press an element and a ripple spreads out from the point you pressed. Hold it and the ripple stays; let go and it fades. It's drawn beneath the element's content, so the text on it stays exactly as readable as it was.

Shapes

Fill, ring, soft, rings

The four built-in shapes. Press anywhere in each pad. The Shape control in the panel puts every element on the page on one of them, and Ripple.addShape() adds your own.

shape: 'fill'
shape: 'ring'
shape: 'soft'
shape: 'rings'

Past the edge

Rings around icons

A ripple clips to the element by default. Small round buttons look better with contained: false, a ring that spreads past the edge, and origin: 'center', so it's always centered however the icon was pressed.

Color

Its own color, or yours

A ripple takes the element's text color, so it reads on light and dark buttons alike without being told. color sets it outright.
