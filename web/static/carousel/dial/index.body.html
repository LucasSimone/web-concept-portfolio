Dial

The View-Master reel itself, not the viewer. Cards mount radially around a hub on the bottom edge, so the top half of the disc arcs across the window. Twelve o'clock is the gate, and since a radial card is only upright at the top of its arc, the selected one is the only card the right way up. Grab the face anywhere and turn it like a knob.

Lift the hub into the box

The whole disc at once

The half circle is only where pivot sits by default: on the bottom edge, so exactly half the reel is in the window. Move the hub up into the box and the rest of the disc follows, until every card is in view and the arc closes into the reel as you'd hold it. Same mechanism, same rotational drag — only the center moved.

Text, images, video — anything

Cards are just content

The mechanism only touches a card's transform and opacity; what goes inside is yours — a paragraph, an <img>, a video, a pull quote, a link. Bigger cards want a bigger disc and a wider cardAngle so they still sit apart. The link on the last card works once that card reaches the gate; before that, clicking spins it there first.

One disc, no two alike

They don't have to match

Each card's distance from the hub comes from its own height, so a disc takes whatever mix of sizes you give it and every card still sits flush to the rim — taller ones reach further in. Nothing on this disc shares a width or a height with anything else.
