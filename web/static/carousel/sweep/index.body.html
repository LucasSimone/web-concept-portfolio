Wheel, drag, or hover

Sweep

A strip of cards you browse by wheel, drag, or by moving the pointer across it. The focused card travels the full width of the window as you work through the list, its neighbors packing edge to edge with no gaps as they shrink away behind it. Stop moving and it snaps.

01

Wheel

Scroll over the strip to move through it — either axis, whichever the input carries more of.

02

Drag

Press and pull. Release goes into a damped coast that always settles exactly on a card.

03

Hover

With enough cards on screen, moving the pointer across the strip pulls the nearest one into focus.

04

Settle

A card at rest in focus fires sweep-settle — the hook a page uses to reset a preview.

05

Falloff

Distance from focus drives scale on an exponential curve, never opacity.

06

Packed

Every card's position is the cumulative sum of its neighbors' half-widths — no gaps, ever.

07

Memory

Leave and come back — sessionStorage remembers which card was focused.

Under five cards

Hover-follow turns off

Below five visible cards, moving the pointer no longer pulls one to center: with this few it feels like the strip is fighting you rather than helping. Wheel and drag work at any count — try them here.
