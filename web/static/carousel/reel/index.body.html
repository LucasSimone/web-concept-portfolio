Wheel, drag, click, or keys

Reel

A View-Master-style carousel. Cards ride a ring that curves away behind one focus point, dead center. The focused card grows to full size exactly as it arrives — a slide held up to the eye — while the rest fall into a halo behind it, smaller and fainter the further round they sit. Or leave it turning on its own.

01

Focus

The focused card sits dead center at full size. It reaches zero distance from center exactly as it comes into focus, instead of arriving at a separate slot.

02

Ring

Every other card sits on a real ring behind it at a constant distance from center. Only the one settling into focus moves inward.

03

Wheel + Drag

A scroll or a drag spins the ring. wheelAxis picks which scroll axis it takes — both, or only one, so page scroll can pass straight through.

04

Click + Keys

Click any card behind the focus to spin it to the front; the front card's own links then work normally. Arrow keys step it one at a time.

05

Settle

A card at rest in focus fires reel-settle and carries .is-focused — reset a preview animation from it, same hook as Sweep's.

06

Size

ringSize scales the ring against the box it's measured in; cardAngle sets how tightly packed it is.

07

Loop or Ends

A closed loop by default — spin either way as far as you like. Turn loop off and it becomes an arc with a first and last card.
