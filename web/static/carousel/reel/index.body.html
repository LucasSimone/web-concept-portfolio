Wheel, drag, click, or keys

Reel

A View-Master-style carousel — cards sit on a ring that curves back and away behind a single focus point, dead center. The focused card grows to full size exactly as it arrives there, like holding a slide up to the eye; the rest curve off into a halo behind it, shrinking and fading the further round they sit. Spin it with the wheel, drag it, click a card to bring it to the front, walk it with the arrow keys, or leave it turning by itself.

01

Focus

The focused card sits dead center at full size — it shrinks to zero distance from center exactly as it comes into focus, rather than arriving at a separate center slot.

02

Ring

Every other card sits on a real ring behind it, at a constant distance from center — only the one settling into focus moves inward.

03

Wheel + Drag

A scroll or a drag spins the ring. wheelAxis picks which scroll axis it takes — both, or only one, so page scroll can pass straight through.

04

Click + Keys

Click any card behind the focus to spin it to the front; the front card's own links then work normally. Arrow keys step it one at a time.

05

Settle

A card at rest in focus fires reel-settle and carries .is-focused — reset a preview animation from it, same hook as Sweep's.

06

Size

ringSize scales the ring against the box it's measured in; cardAngle sets how tightly packed it is.

07

Loop or Ends

A closed loop by default — spin either way as far as you like. Turn loop off and it becomes an arc with a first and last card.

Cards are just content

A card is whatever markup you put inside .reel-card — the mechanism only ever touches its transform and opacity. Here the same ring holds an image swatch and a video placeholder instead of text.
