Gears

Trains of gears whose teeth really mesh, so turning one turns the rest: small gears race, big ones crawl, and each turns against its neighbor. Some trains have a motor, marked by a filled axle, and the rest wait to be moved. Grab a gear and drag it round, or sweep the cursor across one to flick it.
