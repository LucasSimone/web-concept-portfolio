Background 05 — Interactive

Outer Worlds

A randomly generated planet that turns under the cursor and coasts to a stop when you let go, with no edge and no seam to reach in any direction. The same world draws three ways — halftone Dots, a relief Wireframe, or a filled Map — as a globe or as an endless flat plane.

Applied to a smaller element

Same class, same script — its own planet, from its own seed.

Zoom is set relative to the element's diagonal, so a small box frames the same amount of world as the hero.
