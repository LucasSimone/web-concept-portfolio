Background 05 — Interactive

Outer Worlds

A planet generated at random, turning under the cursor and coasting to a stop when you let go. There is no edge or seam to reach in any direction. The same world draws three ways — halftone Dots, relief Wireframe, or filled Map — as a globe or as a flat plane that never ends.

Applied to a smaller element

Same class, same script — its own planet, from its own seed.

Zoom follows the element's diagonal, so a small box frames as much world as the wide one above.
