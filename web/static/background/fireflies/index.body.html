Background 04 — Continuous

Fireflies

Soft glowing points wander an open field on their own slowly-turning paths, each blinking independently — long dark stretches punctuated by a brief rise and fall in brightness, occasionally stuttering into a quick double-flash. Every firefly carries its own small variation on the base glow color and twinkles slightly in size as it brightens, so the field reads as a population rather than one shape repeated. Nothing responds to the cursor; it just keeps drifting. Warm yellow-green by default — the control panel's color picker can flatten it to solid black or anything else.
