Background 04 — Continuous

Fireflies

Glowing points drift across an open field on slowly turning paths, each blinking on its own clock: long dark stretches, a brief swell of light, now and then a stuttered double flash. No two share quite the same color or size, so the field looks like a population instead of one shape copied. The cursor does nothing here. Warm yellow-green by default, and the color picker will take it anywhere, including flat black.
