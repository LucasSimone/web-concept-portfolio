Background 06 — Continuous

Falling Leaves

Leaves come down the way real ones do: swinging side to side, hanging for a moment at the end of each swing, and turning over now and then to show a darker underside. Far leaves are smaller, slower and fainter, so the field has depth. Every so often a gust rolls in from one side and pushes them along. Change the shapes and colors and the same motion works for petals or confetti.
