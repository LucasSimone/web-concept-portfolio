Background 03 — Interactive

Vacuum

Bring the cursor near anything in the section below — text, the swatch, the thick border — and it sheds dust toward you. Each speck takes the color of the thing it came off, read pixel by pixel in the case of the image.

Get close and this starts shedding dust

A thick black border, also made of dust

Hover the left edge of the swatch above, then the right. The dust changes color because it is sampled at that spot, not averaged across the whole image.

Applied to smaller elements

Plain text in a bordered box — both the border and the letters shed dust.

Ambient dust

Hover the black border to watch it shed. The open field sheds too, in the panel's own background color — invisible, because there is nothing for it to contrast against.
