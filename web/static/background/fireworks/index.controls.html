?When on, clicking anywhere in a sky sends a rocket up from the bottom edge, from somewhere near below that spot, to burst right where you clicked, on top of the automatic launches. Clicks on links and buttons inside it are left alone. Off makes the sky ignore the mouse entirely.
Background ?The sky color. A dark one switches to glowing light: overlapping bursts add up brighter, particles run whiter as they burn, and the text on top turns white. A light one draws the bursts as paint instead, with particles running darker. Pick burst colors that stand out against it.
?Specific makes every burst exactly one of the colors below. Range treats them as a gradient, left to right, and each burst can land anywhere along it. With two or more colors, about a quarter of bursts are two-tone, split down the middle at a random angle.
Colors ?The burst colors, from one up to six. + adds a swatch on the end and − removes the last one. The strip underneath shows what you'll get: separate bands for Specific, a smooth blend for Range. Within a burst, each particle is that color or a version 20% or 40% of the way to white (on a dark sky) or black (on a light one), so it glitters.
