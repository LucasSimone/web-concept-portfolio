Background 07 — Interactive

Fireworks

Rockets go up from the bottom at random, slow as they climb, and burst at the top of their arc. The sparks spread out, slow down, droop and fade, and a few twinkle or crackle on the way out. Each burst picks a pattern, from an even ball to long drooping willow trails, and you can write your own. Click anywhere in the sky to send one up to that spot.
