Text Effects Lab

A collection of black & white text animation experiments. Pick a web effect below to see it live and tune its parameters with the on-page controls.

01 — Double Speak — Scroll

Double Speak

A ghost copy of the text trails behind as you scroll, like a pen stroke drawn twice.

View →
02 — Wave Form — Continuous

Wave Form

Each character wriggles in a slow wave, pushing the text into a fluid, drifting rhythm.

View →
03 — Paint Drag — Scroll

Paint Drag

Text smears like wet paint dragged by a brush, streaking and fading as you scroll.

View →
04 — In Flight Out — Scroll

In Flight Out

Letters arrive from scattered paths below, assemble into a phrase, then scatter away above as you keep scrolling.

View →
05 — Triple Vision — Scroll

Triple Vision

Old-school red & cyan 3D-glasses print separation — the channels split apart as you scroll and snap back together when you stop.

View →
06 — Type Pan Vertical — Scroll

Type Pan Vertical

A typewriter caret types out the line as you scroll, panning the window right as the line outgrows it — scroll back to rewind or erase, your choice.

View →
07 — Type Pan — Scroll

Type Pan

Regular mouse-wheel scrolling pans the whole page sideways, typing out a long title as the caret line reaches each letter — reverse to erase or keep it, your choice.
